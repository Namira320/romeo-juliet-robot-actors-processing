JULIET: Hello Romeo, are you there?
ROMEO: Yes Juliet, I am here!
JULIET: I missed you.
ROMEO: I missed you too!
JULIET: Will you stay with me forever?
ROMEO: Yes, my Juliet. Always.
JULIET: Promise me you won’t leave, no matter what happens.
ROMEO: I promise. Even if the stars fall from the sky.
JULIET: Then let’s hold on to this moment.
ROMEO: A moment I wish could last a lifetime.
